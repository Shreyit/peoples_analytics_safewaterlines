# Safewater Lines — assignment brief

Source: the organisation's project communication supplied by the user on 28 October 2026. This file records the assignment requirements; our working approach lives in [PROJECT_BRAIN.md](PROJECT_BRAIN.md).

## Project objective

Transform employee survey data into a meaningful story that helps Safewater Lines understand its people and define a practical pathway for improvement. Survey scores and dashboards are supporting evidence, not the end product.

## Questions the organisation wants answered

- What do the findings reveal about employee experience and organisational culture?
- What strengths should Safewater Lines preserve and build upon?
- Where do gaps, variations, or emerging concerns suggest opportunities for improvement?
- What insights come from employee comments and qualitative feedback?
- How can HR and business leaders translate the findings into measurable interventions?
- What should the organisation prioritise to strengthen engagement, trust, employee experience, and workplace effectiveness?

## Areas of exploration

| Dimension | Focus in the brief |
|---|---|
| Credibility | Trust in leadership, communication, and management practices |
| Respect | Employee support, development, and professional treatment |
| Fairness | Equitable treatment and consistency |
| Pride | Meaningful work, organisational association, and employee advocacy |
| Camaraderie | Collaboration, belonging, and workplace relationships |

The analysis may also explore learning and career growth, resources, overall employee experience, and what leadership could do to help employees feel valued, supported, and respected.

## Expected deliverables

| Deliverable | Required substance |
|---|---|
| Data analysis and insights | Structured analysis of trends, patterns, comparisons, and areas requiring attention |
| Organisational story | A clear narrative supported by evidence rather than assumptions |
| Visualisation and presentation | Charts, dashboards, heatmaps, or other visuals that make findings understandable |
| HR and organisational pathway | Practical, prioritised interventions with ownership, implementation priorities, and measurable outcomes |
| Executive presentation | A concise, professional presentation of the significant findings and proposed way forward |
