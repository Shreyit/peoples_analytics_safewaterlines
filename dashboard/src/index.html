How to read this dashboard

Scope
327 master response records, 30 rating items. Eligible workforce size is unavailable, so no participation rate is calculated.
Measures
Favourable = 4–5; unfavourable = 1–2; 3 means Sometimes. Culture mean averages 25 items and is not a validated engagement index.
Filters
Department and tenure apply to the overview. Focus findings always show the full study. Scores for fewer than ten records are withheld.
Employee voice
Topic counts are unique response records, with multiple topics allowed. They are suggestions, not dissatisfaction or incident counts.
Interpretation
Comments received a documented assistant re-review; independent human validation remains outstanding. Associations are not causal effects.

Aggregate local snapshot · no raw comments, employee names or individual records are included.
For a readable, non-scrolling layout, use a browser viewport of at least 1120 × 640 or enter full screen.
